Recovery Guidelines
  • Sleep 8–10 hours every night
  • Stretch / foam roll after hard sessions
  • Easy days = truly easy effort
  • Communicate pain or fatigue immediately
Fueling Tips
  • Eat carbs + protein within 30–60 min after practice
  • Drink water throughout the day + electrolytes on long runs
  • Never skip breakfast on practice days
  • Avoid heavy/fried food before hard workouts
